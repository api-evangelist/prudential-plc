## PruBSN-Specific Business Context

### Takaful Terminology
- **Takaful**: Islamic insurance based on Shariah principles (mutual cooperation and protection)
- **Takaful Certificate**: Policy document (not "insurance policy")
- **Takaful Participant**: Policyholder/customer
- **Takaful Consultant/Agent**: Sales representative
- **Contribution**: Premium payment
- **Tabarru'**: Donation/contribution to the common fund
- **Wakalah**: Agency contract model
- **Hibah**: Gift/bonus from surplus
- **Shariah Compliance**: Adherence to Islamic law principles

### Product Categories
1. **Protection Plans**: Death, disability, critical illness coverage
2. **Health Plans**: Medical and hospitalization coverage
3. **Wealth Plans**: Savings and investment-linked takaful
4. **Riders**: Supplementary benefits added to basic plans
5. **Takafulink**: Unit-linked investment plans

### Key Products & Services
- **Basic Plans**: Core takaful certificates
- **Medical Riders**: PRUMedik, PRUMedik Asas, PRUCancer Cover Plus
- **Panel Hospitals & Clinics**: Network of healthcare providers
- **PRUPanel Plus**: Enhanced hospital panel benefits
- **i-Lindung**: EPF-linked protection scheme
- **SIKR**: Skim Insurans Kesejahteraan Rakyat (government welfare scheme)
- **Takaful Calculator**: Online quotation tool (https://prubsnadvisor.com)
- **Fund Price**: Daily unit pricing for investment-linked funds

### Digital Platforms
- **PruBSN Touch**: Mobile app for certificate management
- **PRUServices**: Web portal for participant self-service
- **PruBSN Pay**: Online payment platform (https://app.prubsn.com.my/PruBSNPay/)
- **MobileME**: Mobile Medical Examination service

### Life Stages Framework
Content organized around customer life stages:
- Single/Young Adult
- New Parents
- Growing Family
- Mature Family
- Towards Retirement

### Value-Added Services
- **Islamic Spiritual Support**: Collaboration with Darussyifa'
- **Second Medical Opinion**: Healthcare consultation service
- **Day Surgery Benefit**: Outpatient surgical procedures coverage
- **PruBSN Prihatin**: Charitable/CSR arm
