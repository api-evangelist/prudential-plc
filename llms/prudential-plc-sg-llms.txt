# Prudential Singapore – llms.txt
> Prudential Singapore is one of Singapore's leading life insurance companies, serving the financial and protection needs of Singaporeans for over 85 years. The company offers health insurance, life insurance, wealth accumulation, and legacy planning solutions for individuals, families, and enterprises. Prudential Singapore connects individuals and families with comprehensive insurance and financial planning solutions, while also offering group employee benefit plans for corporate clients.
